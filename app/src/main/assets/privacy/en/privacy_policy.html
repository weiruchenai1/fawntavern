Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026
Effective date: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the FawnTavern application and its local workspace features. FawnTavern is a chat tool based primarily on local storage and does not require you to register an account. Information is processed in a lawful, fair, necessary, and transparent manner, and your explicit consent is obtained where required.

1. Information We Collect and Process

1. Local workspace content. This includes chat messages, conversation titles, character cards, lorebooks, presets, images, attachments, text-to-speech settings, search settings, and app preferences that you create, enter, import, or save. This content is stored only on your device by default.

2. Service configuration and credentials. This includes API addresses, model names, request parameters, and access keys that you provide. They are used to connect to the AI, search, or speech services you select and are stored locally as required by app features.

3. Network requests you initiate. When you send a message, search, generate speech, load a remote image, or use another network feature, the app sends the input required to complete that request to the service address you selected. Requests may include URLs, headers, network metadata, and content you submitted. The recipient determines its own processing and retention practices.

4. Device and app information. The app may process the Android version, device model, app version, and runtime state for interface compatibility, troubleshooting, and secure operation. This information is not intentionally uploaded unless you consent to the relevant feature.

5. Crash reports, security logs, and usage statistics. Crash reports and security logs are generated and stored locally by default. Remote diagnostics and usage statistics operate only after you accept this Privacy Policy and while the corresponding setting is enabled. You can disable this feature in Settings.

Remote diagnostics upload notice. Depending on device region and release configuration, the app uses Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Bugly for remote diagnostics. Information that may be uploaded includes app instance identifiers, device identifiers, device model and manufacturer, operating system and network information, IP address, app package and version, build type, crash stack traces, thread and runtime state, diagnostic logs, and app start events. To select a regional diagnostics service, the app may also use Cloudflare to obtain a country or region code associated with the IP address. The app does not intentionally include chat content, attachment contents, or API keys as remote diagnostics fields.

6. Plugin data. If you install or enable a plugin, the app reads its scripts, manifest, and the input needed to run it locally. Whether a plugin accesses the network and what information it processes depend on the plugin and your configuration.

2. Why We Process Information

We process the information above to save and display your workspace, complete AI, search, speech, image, and plugin requests you initiate, provide backup and import/export features, improve compatibility and stability, and help troubleshoot issues you choose to report.

Except where necessary to complete a request you initiate, comply with law, or protect app security, the app does not sell chat content, use it for advertising profiles, or intentionally provide it to unrelated third parties.

3. Third-Party Services and Sharing

The app does not itself operate AI, search, or speech services. When you configure and use a third-party service, relevant input is sent to that provider. The provider may process IP addresses, account identifiers, request content, and network logs under its own rules.

The components, providers, processed fields, and purposes used by the app are described in the in-app Third-Party Information Sharing List. Apart from network requests you initiate, the app does not intentionally share chat content with these services.

4. Storage Location and Retention

Local workspaces, configuration, credentials, and logs generally remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. You control exported backup files. Information sent to a third-party service is stored where and for as long as that provider determines. Review a provider's rules before configuring its service.

The app does not create a remote FawnTavern account or a centralized chat database for this Policy. Optional remote diagnostics and statistics are processed only as needed while the relevant feature is enabled.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You can access, modify, export, or delete workspaces and configuration stored on your device. You can disable remote diagnostics, usage statistics, and other optional features, and you may withdraw your privacy consent. Use Settings > Data Management to inspect, export, delete, or clear data.

After consent is withdrawn, the app stops statistics and diagnostics that require consent. This does not prevent you from managing data already stored locally. You may also uninstall the app or clear its app data to remove local content.

6. Information Security

The app uses local file isolation, access controls, protected temporary files, and a safe import recovery process to reduce the risk of disclosure, accidental deletion, and corruption. Protect your device, API keys, and exported backups, and do not leave unencrypted credentials or backup files on a public device.

7. Children

If you are below the legal age of majority, use this app only with guidance from a parent or guardian. Guardians should help minors manage chat content, network requests, and plugin permissions responsibly.

8. Policy Updates

If app features, data processing practices, or legal requirements change materially, this Policy will be updated and the app will ask for your confirmation again where appropriate. The updated version will show new update and effective dates.

9. Contact

The app currently has no in-app privacy request form. If you have questions about this Policy, data processing, or privacy rights, contact the maintainer through the contact method provided by the distribution channel from which you obtained the app.
